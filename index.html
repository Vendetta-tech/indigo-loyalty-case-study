The Challenge & Opportunity

🎯 The Problem

Growing a loyalty program goes beyond sign-ups. The real challenge is transforming new members into active, repeat customers while building meaningful partner touchpoints between transactions.

Most programs measure success by volume alone, missing the deeper opportunity to understand member quality and lifetime value.

Business Questions

Which acquisition channels bring the most valuable members?

How can campaigns move members through the full funnel—from awareness to conversion?

Which segments and partners deserve strategic focus and investment?

How do we keep members engaged between flight purchases?

Critical Insights

📈 Volume is Incomplete

Measure each channel from sign-up through activation, repeat activity, and conversion—not just acquisition numbers.

🎯 Build Funnels

Reach, engagement, cost, and value must sit together in one scorecard for clarity and accountability.

🔗 Partners Create Habits

Everyday spend, banking, and travel partners keep loyalty active between flight purchases.

👥 Segment Early

New members, infrequent travellers, and VFFs need different journeys, messaging, and investment.

Partner Ecosystem Strategy

🏦 Banking

Role: Acquire members and encourage repeat earning through co-branded products.

🛍️ Everyday Spend

Role: Create regular touchpoints between trips with relevant, habitual purchases.

✈️ Travel & Stays

Role: Extend the travel journey and bundle value beyond flight redemption.

🏪 Airport Retail

Role: Activate high-intent moments around travel with relevant offers.

Strategic Recommendations

01. Measure Quality

Track each source from sign-up through activation, repeat activity, and conversion to reveal true member value.

02. Segment Strategically

Differentiate journeys for new members, infrequent travellers, and high-value frequent fliers.

03. Activate Partners

Create regular touchpoints through everyday-spend and travel-adjacent partnerships.

04. Build Scorecards

Review member activity, volume, engagement, and commercial value together quarterly.
