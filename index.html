Independent loyalty analytics case study

How could BluChip improve member acquisition and engagement through smarter campaigns and partnerships?

A portfolio case study exploring acquisition channels, customer segments, campaign performance and partner ecosystems using a simulated 1,000-member dataset.

Transparency: Publicly available information was researched to understand the IndiGo BluChip ecosystem. Customer-level data and performance figures used in the analysis are simulated for portfolio and learning purposes and are not IndiGo internal data.

The problem

Growing a loyalty program is not only about getting more sign-ups. The harder problem is turning new members into active, repeat customers while creating useful partner touchpoints between trips.

Business question

How can a loyalty program improve new member acquisition and existing-member engagement through targeted campaigns and strategic partner collaborations?

What I wanted to learn

Which acquisition channels bring valuable members? Which campaigns move people from exposure to conversion? Which segments and partners deserve more attention?

My approach

01. Map the public program context

Reviewed publicly available BluChip information, membership benefits and partner categories.

02. Create an analytical sandbox

Created a simulated dataset of 1,000 member records because internal member data is not public.

03. Build a reporting framework

Tracked acquisition, engagement, conversion, campaign cost, estimated revenue impact and partner activity.

04. Turn analysis into decisions

Used the results to propose segmentation, campaign and partner-management recommendations.

Using a funnel makes it easier to compare channels and campaigns by the quality of member behaviour, not only sign-up volume.

What I measured

  • Acquisition channels

  • Estimated revenue impact

Key insights from the simulated analysis

Volume is not enough

Acquisition sources should be measured through downstream activation and conversion, not only member sign-ups.

Campaigns need a funnel

Reach, engagement, conversion, cost and estimated value should sit together in one scorecard.

Partners can create habits between trips

Everyday spend, banking and travel partners can keep a loyalty relationship active between flight purchases.

Segment before sending

New members, infrequent travellers and high-value frequent travellers should not receive identical journeys.

Partner ecosystem lens

Banking

Role: Acquire members and encourage repeat earning.

Everyday spend

Role: Create regular engagement between trips.

Travel and stays

Role: Extend the travel journey and bundle value.

Airport retail

Role: Activate relevant moments around travel.

Recommendations I would test

01. Measure acquisition quality

Track each source from sign-up through activation, repeat activity and conversion.

02. Segment before sending

Differentiate journeys for new members, infrequent travellers and high-value frequent travellers.

03. Use partners between trips

Create more regular touchpoints through relevant everyday-spend and travel-adjacent partnerships.

04. Create a joint partner scorecard

Review member activity, transaction volume, campaign engagement and estimated commercial value together.
