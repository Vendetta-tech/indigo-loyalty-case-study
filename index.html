The Business Challenge

🎯 The Core Problem

Growing a loyalty program extends far beyond acquiring new sign-ups. The true challenge lies in converting members into active, engaged repeat customers while building strategic touchpoints between flight purchases.

Most programs measure success by volume, missing critical insights about member quality and lifetime value.

❓ Key Questions

  • Which channels attract high-value members?
  • How do campaigns drive full-funnel conversion?
  • Which segments & partners warrant strategic focus?
  • How to maintain engagement between transactions?

Strategic Insights

📈 Volume is Incomplete

Measure each channel from sign-up through activation, repeat engagement, and conversion—not acquisition numbers alone.

🎯 Build Funnels

Reach, engagement, cost, and value must sit together in one scorecard for accountability and strategic decisions.

🔗 Partners Create Habits

Everyday spend, banking, and travel partners keep loyalty active between flights through consistent touchpoints.

👥 Segment Early

New members, infrequent travelers, and VFFs require different journeys, messaging, and investment strategies.

Partner Ecosystem Strategy

🏦 Banking Partnerships

Role: Acquire members and drive repeat earning through co-branded credit products and benefits.

🛍️ Everyday Spend

Role: Create habitual touchpoints between trips with retail, dining, and lifestyle partners.

✈️ Travel & Hospitality

Role: Extend travel journey and bundle value beyond flight redemption with stays and experiences.

🏪 Airport Retail

Role: Activate high-intent moments around travel with relevant, timely offers and products.

Strategic Recommendations

01. Measure Quality

Track each acquisition source from sign-up through activation, repeat usage, and conversion to reveal true member value.

02. Segment Precision

Differentiate journeys for new members, infrequent travelers, and high-value frequent flyers with tailored messaging.

03. Activate Partners

Create regular engagement touchpoints through everyday-spend and travel-adjacent partnerships between flights.

04. Build Scorecards

Review member activity, transaction volume, engagement, and commercial value together in quarterly business reviews.
