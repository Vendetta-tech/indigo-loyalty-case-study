# IndiGo BluChip Loyalty Growth & Campaign Analytics

## Independent Portfolio Case Study | Omkar Pawar

### Project Overview

This independent portfolio case study explores how a loyalty program could improve member acquisition, engagement, campaign performance, and partner collaboration through structured data analysis and business recommendations.

The project demonstrates my approach to loyalty analytics, campaign reporting, customer segmentation, and MIS reporting.

---

## Business Question

**How can a loyalty program improve new member acquisition and existing-member engagement through targeted campaigns and strategic partner collaborations?**

---

## Project Approach

### 1. Research

Reviewed publicly available information to understand the IndiGo BluChip loyalty ecosystem, membership benefits, and partner categories.

### 2. Data Analysis

Created and analysed a simulated dataset of 1,000 loyalty members to explore:

- Member acquisition
- Customer engagement
- Campaign performance
- Conversion
- Partner activity
- Estimated revenue impact

### 3. MIS Reporting

Built a structured reporting framework to track important loyalty KPIs and support business decision-making.

### 4. Business Recommendations

Used the analysis to develop recommendations related to:

- Customer segmentation
- Campaign targeting
- Acquisition quality
- Member engagement
- Partner ecosystem opportunities

---

## Key Insights

- Acquisition performance should be measured beyond the number of member sign-ups.
- Campaign performance should be evaluated across the complete funnel, from reach and engagement to conversion.
- Different customer segments may require different communication and engagement strategies.
- Strategic partners can create additional loyalty touchpoints between customer trips.
- A structured KPI dashboard can help teams monitor loyalty performance and identify improvement opportunities.
